Your automations

An automation keeps your devices as you say while its conditions hold, and puts them back when they stop holding. It acts on its own, so it works even when “Allow device changes” is off on the Controls page. It does nothing when the data it needs is missing or old. If you want to change a setting by hand, Take over that automation first, or it will put the setting back.
